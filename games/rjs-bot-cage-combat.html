How to play

Drive

One player: W A S D or arrows to drive, Space fires your weapons, Shift is your special.

Same keyboard: P1 uses W A S D, Space, Left Shift. P2 uses arrows, Enter, Right Shift.

C switches camera. Esc pauses.

Build

Every bot gets 30 build points. Bodies, parts and upgrades all cost points, and parts add weight the body has to carry.

Spinners and drums are always on and spin up on their own. Flippers, hammers and lifters fire when you press. Saws, crushers and flames run while you hold.

Win

Every fight is 1 v 1. Knock out the other bot: destroy it, drop it in a pit, or flip it and let the 10 count run out.

If time runs out, the judges score the bot with the most health left and the most damage dealt.

Flipped? Invertible bodies keep driving. Otherwise you need a self-righter or a flipper to get back up.

Party

Host a party room and share the code. Up to 8 drivers join, the bracket is drawn at random, and empty spots are filled with CPU bots. Last bot standing takes the crown.
