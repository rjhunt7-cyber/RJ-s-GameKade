How to eat

Chomp lunges your predator's neck out and snaps. Tap it for a quick bite, or hold it to park your mouth open like a trap and let go to snap. Up to 3 critters per bite, and 3 or more in one gulp earns +2.

Points: mouse and frog 1, rabbit, chicken and duckling 2, piglet and lamb 3, gazelle 4, golden goose 10. Skip the skunk (you get stunned and stink) and the porcupine (minus 3 and a sore snout).

Special fires when your meter is full. Vinnie lobs a decoy carrot and reels the mob in. Gus inhales everything in front of him. Dale fires a rocket fork that kebabs up to two critters (and sometimes explodes). Lou's laugh dizzies every rival and pulls prey toward him.

Crates parachute in. They slide around the arena like marbles. Chomp one for Mega Maw, Meat Magnet, Chili Tongue, or a Stink Bomb that makes prey run from the leader. Tunnels link by color, bumpers ricochet critters, and stampedes charge straight through everyone's reach.

Keys: solo and online use Space or Z to chomp, X or Shift for special, or left and right mouse. Party: P1 Z/X, P2 Q/W, P3 O/P, P4 Left/Right arrows. Gamepads: A chomps, B special. Esc pauses.
